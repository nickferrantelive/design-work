## What a good entry answers

**Research**
- What does the tool do, in one plain sentence?
- Who makes it, and how mature is it (beta, launched, funded)?
- Price and free tier, checked on the tool's own site.
- What it does better than what Nick already uses, and where it falls short.
- A verdict: strong, weak or mixed, with the reason.

**Roadmap**
- One recommendation first: adopt, try or skip.
- Steps to try it, cheapest and free first.
- Which of Nick's projects it would serve, and how.
- What would make the verdict change.
